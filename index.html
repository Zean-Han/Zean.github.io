About

Welcome to my homepage.

My name is Zean Han, a Ph.D. student in the Department of Industrial Engineering and Decision Analytics (IEDA), HKUST. I am fortunate to be advised by Prof. Jiheng Zhang, Department Head and Full Professor in IEDA. I received my M.Phil. in Mathematics from The Chinese University of Hong Kong (CUHK).

My research sits at the intersection of statistics, operations research, and machine learning. In particular, I focus on (1) queueing models and control for improving LLM inference efficiency, and (2) sample-efficient reinforcement learning algorithms.

You can reach me at zeanhan01@gmail.com.
